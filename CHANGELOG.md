## 1.0.0-rc.1 — 2026-09-15

Atlas V1 release candidate for the October 18, 2026 target.

### Included

- Four bilingual GED comparison lessons covering more, fewer, difference, and compare-unknown problems.
- Persistent per-lesson progress, attempts, completion, language, accessibility preferences, and cumulative history.
- Separate current and prior completed attempts with safe malformed/versioned-data recovery.
- Generated English/Spanish flashcards, worksheets, quizzes, answer keys, structured data, and traceability hashes.
- Keyboard, screen-reader, responsive, reduced-motion, forced-color, and dialog-focus hardening.
- Automated curriculum, persistence, analytics, bilingual, accessibility, release-route, link, import, and asset checks.

### Release boundary

Accounts, cloud sync, teacher dashboards, native mobile apps, Atlas Studio, and curriculum beyond Lessons 001–004 are not part of V1.
