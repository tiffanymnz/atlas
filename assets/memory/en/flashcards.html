MATH-NS-COMP-0001 · comparison.more

How many more?

Learning goal: Understand that 'how many more' asks for the gap between two amounts.

Remember: Larger amount − smaller amount = difference

Recall: When a problem asks how many more one amount is than another, what should you find?

Answer: The gap between the amounts

MATH-NS-COMP-0002 · comparison.fewer

How many fewer?

Learning goal: Understand that 'how many fewer' asks for the same gap from the other direction.

Remember: Larger amount − smaller amount = how many fewer

Recall: Choose the rule that works for a how-many-fewer comparison.

Answer: Find the gap from larger to smaller

MATH-NS-COMP-0003 · difference

Difference

Learning goal: Understand that difference names the gap between two amounts.

Remember: Difference = the gap between two amounts

Recall: The difference between two amounts is...

Answer: the gap between them

MATH-NS-COMP-0004 · compare_unknown

Find the compared amount

Learning goal: Use a known larger amount and known difference to find the unknown smaller amount.

Remember: Known larger amount − known gap = missing smaller amount

Recall: You know the larger amount and the gap. What should you do?

Answer: Subtract the gap from the larger amount
